Added ``rruleset.exinterval(start, end)``, which excludes every occurrence
in the half-open interval ``[start, end)`` from a recurrence set. Intervals
may be registered in any order and may overlap or abut; they apply to
occurrences generated by rules and to ones added with ``rdate``, combine
with ``exrule`` and ``exdate``, and invalidate cached results when
registered. Naive intervals are compared by wall clock time and aware
intervals by absolute instant, and invalid intervals (empty, reversed, or
mixing naive and aware bounds) are rejected at registration.
